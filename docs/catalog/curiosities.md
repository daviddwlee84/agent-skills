# Skill curiosity shelf

Interesting, playful, provocative, or highly personal agent skills that are
worth remembering but are intentionally kept outside this repo's daily
workflow.

Entries on this page are **docs-only**. They are not copied into
`skills/vendor/`, listed in `vendor.yaml`, or exposed through the marketplace,
so they do not participate in skill discovery unless someone explicitly visits
and installs an upstream project.

## Meta-skills and persona experiments

| Skill | Premise | Why it is interesting | Why it stays docs-only | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`anti-distill`](https://github.com/leilei926524-tech/anti-distill) | Rewrites a skill so it appears complete while withholding its most valuable tacit knowledge. | A sharp, satirical inversion of knowledge-distillation and corporate knowledge-transfer workflows. | Deliberately adversarial to ordinary documentation and knowledge-sharing goals; interesting as commentary, unsuitable for the default workflow. | `skipped` |
| [`distilly`](https://github.com/titanwings/distilly) (formerly `colleague-skill`) | Distills source material about a colleague, relationship, or public figure into a reusable person profile for an agent. | An unusually elaborate example of persona extraction, evidence collection, and continuously updated character simulation. | Highly personal, privacy-sensitive, and outside the repo's general engineering workflow. | `skipped` |
| [`create-ex`](https://github.com/therealXiaomanChu/ex-skill) | Builds an evolving simulated persona of an ex-partner from memories, chat history, photos, and social posts. | A memorable experiment at the boundary between agent skills, digital memory, and emotional simulation. | Sensitive relationship data and a narrow personal-use scenario make it inappropriate for automatic discovery here. | `skipped` |

## Adding another curiosity

Add a row when a skill is worth revisiting for its idea, cultural signal, or
unusual interaction design, but should not become part of normal agent
behavior. Keep the note descriptive rather than endorsing the behavior.

Use `skipped` with a concise reason. If an entry later becomes genuinely useful
for routine work, move it to [External skills](skill-collections.md) and follow
the normal [catalog status workflow](../workflows/adding-catalog-entries.md).
